Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 August 2026

This policy explains what Health Wellness collects, why, and what you can do about it. It applies to healthwellness.com and covers visitors from anywhere in the world.

In plain terms

This site does not ask you to create an account and does not sell your data. It uses advertising and analytics services that set cookies, and it collects your email address only if you type it into a signup form. You can opt out of the advertising cookies.

1. Who is responsible

Health Wellness is operated by an individual publisher. For any privacy question, or to exercise any of the rights described below, use the contact page.

2. Information collected automatically

Like most websites, this one and its service providers record certain technical information whenever a page is requested. This typically includes your IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device type, the referring page, the pages you view, and the date and time of the request. This information is used to keep the site working, understand which content is useful, and detect abuse. It is not used to identify you personally.

3. Information you provide

You are only asked for personal information in two places:

  • The email signup form. If you subscribe, your email address is stored by the email service provider and used to send the newsletter. Every email includes a one-click unsubscribe link.
  • The contact form. Your name, email address and message are passed to a third-party form provider and forwarded to the site owner, so a reply can be sent.

Neither is sold, rented or shared for anyone else's marketing.

4. Cookies

A cookie is a small text file stored by your browser. This site and its partners use them for a few purposes:

  • Essential cookies keep basic site functions working.
  • Analytics cookies record aggregate usage — which pages get read, roughly where visitors come from — so content decisions are informed by something other than guessing.
  • Advertising cookies are set by advertising partners to serve and measure ads, and in some cases to personalise them.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking advertising and analytics cookies will not stop the site working.

5. Advertising

This site is supported by display advertising. Third-party advertising vendors, which may include Google AdSense and other ad networks, may serve ads on these pages.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can opt out of a broader set of third-party vendors at aboutads.info or Your Online Choices.
  • Third-party ad networks have their own privacy policies governing the data they collect. This site has no access to, and no control over, cookies set by those networks.

6. Analytics

Aggregate analytics are used to understand which articles are read and how visitors arrive. Where a provider supports it, IP anonymisation is enabled. Analytics data is reviewed in aggregate and is not used to build a profile of any individual reader.

7. Affiliate links

Some equipment posts contain affiliate links. If you buy something through one, this site may earn a small commission at no additional cost to you. Clicking an affiliate link may cause the retailer to set a cookie recording the referral. Affiliate relationships never determine which products are recommended, and posts say plainly when a purchase is not worth making.

8. Legal bases for processing (UK & EU / GDPR)

If you are in the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area, personal data is processed on these bases:

  • Consent — for newsletter emails and for non-essential cookies, including advertising and analytics. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests — for keeping the site secure, preventing abuse, and understanding aggregate usage.
  • Legal obligation — where retention is required by law.

You have the right to access the personal data held about you, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. You also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, use the contact page.

9. Your California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected and for what purpose, to request deletion of personal information, to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. This site does not sell personal information for money. The use of advertising cookies may nonetheless qualify as “sharing” under California law; you can opt out using the advertising controls in section 5, or by contacting the site owner.

10. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in some jurisdictions), and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information here, please get in touch and it will be deleted.

11. Data retention

Email addresses are kept until you unsubscribe or ask for deletion. Contact form messages are kept only as long as needed to handle the correspondence. Analytics and advertising data are retained according to the policies of the respective providers.

12. Security

This site is served over HTTPS and hosts no user accounts, passwords or payment details. No method of transmission or storage on the internet is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

13. International transfers

The service providers used by this site may process data in countries outside your own, including the United States. Where required, those transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

14. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in practice or the law. The date at the top of this page always shows the current version. Material changes will be noted on the site.

15. Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request relating to your data, can be sent through the contact page.
