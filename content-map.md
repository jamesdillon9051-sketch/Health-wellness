## Notes on keyword strategy

- Every primary keyword is a **long-tail phrase**, not a head term. No post targets "weight loss", "home workout" or "build muscle" on their own — those are unwinnable for a new domain.
- Each post also carries 2–3 secondary keywords, assigned at write time from the primary's natural variants (question forms, "for beginners", "at home", "without equipment").
- **Commercial intent** is concentrated in Pillar 4, which is where affiliate revenue would eventually sit. Pillars 1–3 carry the informational volume that earns the domain authority to rank Pillar 4.
- **Ad revenue** is spread across all five: every post has four ad slots (header, in-content, sidebar, footer).

## Cross-pillar linking plan

Each cluster post links to: its two pillar hubs, one sibling cluster, and at least one post in a *different* pillar. Recurring cross-pillar bridges:

- Bodyweight movement posts → `5-minute-warm-up`, `doms-muscle-soreness`
- Quick workout posts → `how-to-structure-a-home-workout`, `weekly-home-workout-schedule`
- Small-space posts → `no-jump-cardio`, `exercise-mat-for-noise-reduction`
- Gear posts → `minimalist-home-gym-guide`, `diy-home-gym-equipment`
- Habit posts → `stick-to-home-workout-habit`, `tracking-progress-at-home`
